# Travel reimbursement policy

## Domestic accommodation

The current domestic hotel reimbursement limit is INR 4500 per night.
Hotel reimbursement requires an itemized receipt and manager approval.
An accommodation expense above INR 4500 per night requires advance approval from the finance director.

## Meals

The current domestic meal reimbursement limit is INR 900 per day.
Alcohol purchases are not reimbursable.

## Claim deadline

Travel reimbursement claims must be submitted within thirty calendar days of returning.
Employees must submit claims through the company expense portal.
Finance processes approved claims within ten business days.
