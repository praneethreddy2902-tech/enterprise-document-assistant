# Compensation review policy

## Review schedule

The annual compensation review takes place in April.
Employees must complete six months of service before they are eligible for an annual compensation review.
A compensation review does not guarantee a salary increase.

## Approval process

Managers submit compensation recommendations to HR by March 15.
Every salary increase requires approval from both the HR director and the finance director.
HR communicates approved salary changes to employees individually.

## Confidential records

Individual salary records are accessible only to authorized HR personnel.
This policy does not contain individual employee salaries.
