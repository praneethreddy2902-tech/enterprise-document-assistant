# Leave policy

## Annual leave

Employees receive twenty days of annual leave per calendar year.
Submit leave requests to your manager at least five working days in advance.

## Sick leave

Employees receive ten days of sick leave per calendar year.
Notify your manager before your scheduled start time when taking sick leave.
