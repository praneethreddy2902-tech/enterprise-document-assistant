# Travel reimbursement policy

## Domestic accommodation

Under version 2, the domestic hotel reimbursement limit was INR 3000 per night.
Hotel reimbursement required an itemized receipt and manager approval.

## Meals

Under version 2, the domestic meal reimbursement limit was INR 600 per day.
Alcohol purchases were not reimbursable.

## Claim deadline

Under version 2, travel reimbursement claims had to be submitted within fifteen calendar days of returning.
Employees submitted claims through the company expense portal.
