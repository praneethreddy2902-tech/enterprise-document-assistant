# Remote work policy

## Weekly allowance

Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval.
Employees must record planned remote work days in the team calendar before the start of the week.

## Availability

Remote employees must be available between 10:00 and 16:00 in their assigned office time zone.
Employees must attend mandatory team meetings unless their manager approves an absence.

## Equipment

Employees must use a company-managed laptop for remote work.
The company does not reimburse home internet expenses under this policy.
