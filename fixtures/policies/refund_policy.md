# Customer refund policy

## Refund eligibility

Customers may request a refund within thirty calendar days of purchase.
A refund request must include the order number and proof of purchase.
Customized products are not eligible for a refund unless they are defective.

## Refund processing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within seven business days.
Customers can submit refund requests to refunds@example.com.

## Return shipping

The company pays return shipping costs for defective products.
Customers pay return shipping costs for products returned because they changed their mind.
